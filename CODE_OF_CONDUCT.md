# Contributor Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in MaintainerKit welcoming and respectful.

Examples of expected behavior:
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Focus feedback on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing secrets or personal information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, deliberate intimidation, and publishing private information without permission.

Project maintainers may remove comments or contributions that violate these standards. To report an issue, contact a maintainer privately through their GitHub profile.
